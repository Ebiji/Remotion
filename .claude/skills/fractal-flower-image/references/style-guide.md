# スタイルガイド（語彙）

## 型
`{scene_core}, {camera}, {background}, rainbow gradient {part}, {water}, {pattern}, ultra detailed, vivid saturated colors, cel-shaded paint texture, 9:16 vertical`

## 語彙
- **花**: iridescent daisy, mandala-petal flower, lotus, gerbera, kaleidoscopic blossom, flowers growing recursively on branches
- **フラクタル**: self-similar branching, Mandelbrot-like filigree, Julia-set tendrils, infinite recursion, Fibonacci spiral, kaleidoscope symmetry, diamond lattice
- **虹グラデ**: rainbow gradient across the petals / trunk / arch (red→orange→yellow→green→cyan→blue→purple)
- **水**: macro water droplets refracting the rainbow, glass-needle rain streaks, giant hanging drop, soap bubbles, polka-dot spheres
- **模様**: paisley swirls, polka dots, mandala medallion
- **背景A**: pure black void / **背景B**: deep saturated blue sky with soft clouds / **背景C**: midnight navy rain
- **カメラ**: worm's-eye view, low-angle macro, top-down macro, one-point perspective, wide-angle, slow zoom into center
- **質感**: cel-shaded, slightly posterized color regions, glossy wet surfaces, high contrast

## 避ける
text, logo, watermark, signature, human figures, muted/pastel palette, blurry, low saturation, photographic realism, grey sky
